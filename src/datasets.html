Data notes

Uneven coverage

Not all countries appear with the same continuity across all series. In many cases, international comparison therefore reflects realistically available coverage rather than perfectly symmetric observations.

Latest available year

The overall window is 2000-2024, but many indicators stop earlier. When charts combine multiple sources, we use the latest available value for each country and flag it to preserve transparency for the user.

Imperfect comparability

Even when two indicators share the same name, data collection processes may differ across countries and years: sample surveys, administrative records, modeled estimates, and methodological revisions.

Observation bias

The most sensitive social datasets tend to underrepresent the most fragile contexts: countries with weak statistical systems, rural areas, marginalized populations, and underreported practices. Missing data therefore does not imply that the phenomenon is absent.

International migration

For migration we use bilateral stocks, not annual flows. This means the chart shows where migrant presence accumulates over time, not the number of departures or arrivals recorded each year.
